# History of how Cloud Computing came to be. 

**not the first distributed system**

Early datacenters date back to the mid 1900s and are some of the early instances of not only distributed systems, but computers all together.
1960s-1970s - Timesharing and data processing industry. Huge computers for processing larges amount of data (for the time)... usually timesheet stuff
Fairly large industry
Honeywell, IBM, Xerox
Some of the hardware fundamentals started here. 

PC in the mid 1980s
grid = coordination system for heterogeneous recourses that are not under a unifed control

Peer-to-peer in the mid 1990s 
    network of symmetric peers
    napster and BitTorrent

Cloud and datacenters started coming back in the late 1990s-early 2000s.
Previous technologies are the backs the giants of modern distributed systems have been built on

### Trends
Doubling periods - exponential growth in hardware capabilities that has been seen throughtout history
    Storage every 12 months
    Bandwidth every 9 months
    CPU every 18 months (this is almost exclusively horizontal now)
