# What is new about modern cloud that distinguises it from systems of old

**four primary topics**
1. Huge scale
2. on-demand access
3. Data-intensive
4. Modern cloud programming paradigms/algorithms/etc.

## Massive scale
100s of thousands of servers
Split into thousands of clusters

## On demand access
No contract is required. 
Anyone can access the compute at any time (anyone at any time applies to public cloud. assuming auth and all that is successful)

## Data intensive
incomprehensible amount of data... "Data numbness"

## Modern programming paradigms
Lots of open source options
Tools, frameworks, ecosystems to work with distributed systems, scale them, and and abstractions on top of them.
Think MapReduce, MangoDB, etc.

## power
Majority Datacenter power come offsite. Hydroelectric, coal, etc.
some have on site powerplants or solarplants. 

## Cooling
Different cooling infrastructures
One that is used is pull air from outside --> combine that air with purified water --> move that cooled air through system
