# Notes on Week7 quiz

- Understand paradigmatism 
    i.e. two words can be substituted for each other.(same semantic or syntactic class).
    high context similarity.
- Understand Syntagmatic words
    i.e. two words that can co-occur (get combined) with each other in the same context. (and have it make sense under the context of the collection). 
    Sequential. Discovered by detecting high co-occurrence in the dataset.

- Be able to parse out the behaviors and attributes of a all the words collected in a co-occurrence window as it relates to a candidate word.

- Understand the different types of sensors, and what makes a sensor subjective?

- Know the text representation spectrum. 

- Know the steps on Natural Language Processing. 
1. POS (part of speech) tagging: assign lexical categorizes (noun, vern, adjective, etc) to tokens
Supplies basic lexical features required by parser
2. syntactic analysis. Determine the grammatical structure.
Scaffolding
3. Semantic analysis: map the syntactic structure on to meaning (word senses, entity types, etc)
the input to interference 
4. Interference: draw conclusions that go beyond literal content. (entailment, question answering, etc.)
