# Notes from the overview (high level) lecture (week 1 first lecture)

## Motivation
### Course revolves around the phenomenon of "big data"
Lots of data being generated. This course focuses on text data. Internet, email, social media, etc. 
Lots of insights but not obvious just by looking at it. Need tech such as data mining into knowledge to support apps and systems.
#### two main types of technologies here
##### text retrieval
initial stage. Reduce total dataset into a small subset that is relevant to question / app. Search or reduction
##### data mining
analying data to obtain knowledge
generally happens after text retrieval.

Then knowledge is derived to support systems.

LLMs and agents (to bring in things like memory) have advanced these two technologies.
