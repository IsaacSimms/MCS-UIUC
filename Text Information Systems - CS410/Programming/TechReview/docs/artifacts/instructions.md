# CS410 Technology Review

The Technology Review is a chance to learn a current technology, application, toolkit, or technique related to text data retrieval and analysis that the course lectures do not cover in detail.

Investigate the topic, review useful resources, and write a short review that helps other CS410 students understand the topic and find good resources for learning more.

The review also becomes part of a growing CS410 technology library on [TextData](https://textdata.org/). Reviews from previous semesters stay available to future students, so the work can help others learn about technologies that may be useful for their course projects.

**Due:** end of Week 11.

The earlier deadline is intentional. The reviews should give access to useful technologies and resources while course project work is still underway.

## What the topic can be

The topic should relate to text data retrieval and analysis. It can fall into one of these broad categories:

- Software tools and toolkits for processing, retrieving, or analyzing text data
- Emerging applications of text retrieval or analysis
- New techniques or methods for text retrieval or analysis

A review can examine:

- One sufficiently substantial toolkit, application, or technique
- Several related tools, applications, or techniques, compared with each other
- A broader technology area from a particular perspective

Examples: a new text retrieval framework, a comparison of several vector databases for a particular use case, how LLMs are being used for information retrieval, or a new technique for analyzing large collections of text.

## Choosing a topic

Choose any topic that is reasonably related to text data retrieval or analysis and appropriate for a Technology Review. No additional approval is required for a topic outside the instructor's suggested list.

Options:

- Use a topic from the suggested topic list.
- Propose a topic based on personal interests or the course project.

A topic that is relevant to the course project is especially useful. Learning the technology through the Tech Review can support better project decisions.

If it is unclear whether a topic fits, ask the instructor or TAs.

### Overlap with existing reviews

Before settling on a topic, search the existing Tech Reviews on TextData. A similar review can still be chosen, but the new review should add something useful. For example:

- Focus on a different perspective or use case
- Include newer resources
- Compare technologies that were not previously compared
- Discuss developments that have appeared since the earlier review
- Provide a more practical analysis or example

Each review should contribute something useful to the CS410 technology library.

## Workflow

### 1. Create a TextData account

If you do not already have a TextData account, visit [https://textdata.org/about](https://textdata.org/about).

Click **Start the Onboarding** to create an account and download the Chrome Extension.

If you already use TextData, skip this step.

### 2. Choose a topic

Choose a topic related to text data retrieval or analysis, either from the suggested list or based on your interests or course project.

No additional approval is required for a self-selected topic, as long as it is reasonably related to the course and appropriate for a Technology Review.

### 3. Search existing Tech Reviews

Explore the public topic communities on [TextData](https://textdata.org/) before creating a community.

Search using keywords related to the topic. Look at existing Tech Reviews to:

- See what previous students have covered
- Avoid unnecessary duplication
- Identify useful resources
- Find opportunities to update or extend an existing review

If the topic substantially overlaps with an existing review, include a link to that review in your own community and explain what your review adds.

### 4. Create the topic community

Create a **public** community on TextData for the Tech Review. Do **not** create it inside the CS410 Fall 2026 community.

1. Click **Communities** in the top-right corner.
2. Click **Create**.
3. Enter the topic as the community name.
4. Click **CREATE**.
5. Find the new community and click the pen icon to edit it.
6. Select **public**.
7. Click **SAVE**.

A brief description of the community is optional.

### 5. Write the topic overview

Create a submission in the topic community titled something like:

> Overview of [Your Topic]

The overview must be at least **100 words** and should:

- Define the topic
- Provide some background
- Explain why the topic is useful

Add the special hashtag `#pin` somewhere in the submission so it appears at the top of the community.

### 6. Collect learning resources

Find and save at least **10** useful web resources related to the topic.

Resources can include:

- Research papers
- Toolkit or project websites
- Official documentation
- Tutorials
- Blog posts
- YouTube videos
- Technical reports
- Practical examples

For each resource, provide a brief explanation of why it is useful. A short paragraph is enough.

Prioritize:

- Authoritative sources
- Technically useful resources
- Recent publications or updates
- Resources that provide different perspectives on the topic

The collection should be useful for someone who wants to learn the topic, rather than simply meeting a number requirement.

The easiest way to save resources is through the TextData Chrome Extension.

### 7. Prepare and record the presentation

Use the collected resources to develop the Technology Review presentation.

The presentation should synthesize what was learned and include original analysis.

Record the presentation as a video and submit the recording according to the course submission instructions.

### 8. Submit the Tech Review to the CS410 class community

Create a submission in the **CS410 Fall 2026** community pointing to the Tech Review community.

The submission must include:

- The URL of the Tech Review community
- The hashtag `#techreview`
- The hashtag `#fa2026`

This lets everyone in the class find the Tech Reviews created by their classmates.

Search for `#techreview` in the class community to see other students' reviews.

## Presentation

The presentation should be a short, focused explanation of what was learned.

A useful structure:

1. **Introduction.** Briefly introduce the topic and explain why it was chosen.
2. **Background.** Explain the problem or need that led to this technology.
3. **How it works.** Explain the main technical ideas in a way that another CS410 student can understand.
4. **Examples or applications.** Show how the technology is used or what problems it can solve.
5. **Comparison, strengths, and limitations.** Discuss important tradeoffs. If appropriate, compare it with another technology or a traditional approach.
6. **Your thoughts.** Share what was learned, what was interesting, and an assessment of the technology.
7. **Resources.** Show the most useful resources and briefly explain why they are recommended.

Cover a smaller number of important ideas clearly and deeply rather than quickly listing many concepts.

Slides, screen demonstrations, diagrams, code examples, or other visual material are fine when they help explain the topic.

There is no strict word-count requirement or page limit. The presentation should be long enough to explain the topic meaningfully without unnecessary filler. As a general guideline, aim for approximately **8–12 minutes**.

## Video requirements

Submit a recorded video presentation of the Technology Review.

The video should:

- Show the presentation materials clearly
- Include a voice explaining the topic
- Demonstrate understanding of the material
- Include original analysis or opinions
- Cite or identify the resources used

Record with any reasonable tool, such as Zoom, PowerPoint, or another screen-recording tool.

The video does not need to be highly polished. What matters is what was learned and how clearly the technology is explained and evaluated.
